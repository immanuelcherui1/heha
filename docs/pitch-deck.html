MovePlanner

PlanningContext → NeedsEvaluation → PlanningRule Observe → evaluate → plan → clarify

17 explicit rules turn inventory/access evidence into preparation tasks and questions for the mover.

Deterministic Jac walkers. No LLM key, invented distance or autonomous booking/payment. The customer approves the quote.

Path to impact

Start with household moves.
Grow with trustworthy operations.

Proposed business model

Explore a disclosed fee on completed marketplace bookings, with optional mover tools after validating demand. Pricing and settlement are future business decisions.

Initial discovery

Interview households, student movers and independent moving teams; pilot in one city before expanding.

Before a live launch

  • Real company and insurance verification
  • M-Pesa reconciliation and settlement
  • Abuse controls and stronger account recovery
  • Reviewed privacy, cancellation and dispute processes
  • Measured service quality and availability
